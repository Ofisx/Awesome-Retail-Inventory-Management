# Awesome-Retail-Inventory-Management

## Top Retail Inventory Management Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Multi-Channel Inventory, Warehouse Stock Control, Order Sync, Manufacturing BOMs, Barcode & Retail Operations*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Retail Inventory Management**. These systems help retailers, ecommerce brands, and wholesalers track stock across channels and locations, manage purchase orders, handle kits/BOMs, sync with sales channels, and maintain accurate on-hand quantities.



**Examples** include Cin7, Unleashed, Zoho Inventory, SkuVault, Katana Cloud Inventory, Fishbowl, Sortly, Finale Inventory, Orderhive, and DEAR Systems / Cin7 Core (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full multi-channel retail inventory platforms with deep marketplace integrations are mostly commercial, but there is a robust open-source ecosystem led by **InvenTree**, complemented by ERP systems with strong inventory modules (**metasfresh**), self-hosted business suites, and specialized stock controllers. This section is heavily expanded with every major active project.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Cin7 (including Cin7 Core / former DEAR Systems)](https://www.cin7.com/)**  

  Leading multi-channel inventory and order management platform for product businesses, with strong warehouse, manufacturing, and ecommerce integrations.



- **[Katana Cloud Inventory](https://katanamrp.com/)**  

  Inventory and manufacturing platform popular with makers and light manufacturers, featuring BOM tracking, production orders, and real-time stock visibility.



- **[Zoho Inventory](https://www.zoho.com/inventory/)**  

  Affordable multi-channel inventory management with order sync, barcoding, and tight integration into the broader Zoho suite; includes a capable free tier.



- **[Unleashed, Fishbowl, Finale Inventory](https://www.unleashedsoftware.com/)**  

  Established inventory and warehouse management solutions serving wholesalers, manufacturers, and multi-location retailers.



- **[SkuVault, Sortly, Orderhive](https://www.skuvault.com/)**  

  Tools focused on warehouse accuracy, simple visual inventory, and multi-channel stock control for growing ecommerce and retail operations.



- **[Other retail & multi-channel inventory platforms](https://www.cin7.com/)**  

  Additional commercial solutions covering demand planning, barcoding, and omnichannel stock synchronization.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[InvenTree](https://github.com/inventree/InvenTree)**  

  Leading open-source inventory management system with powerful low-level stock control, part tracking, plugins, REST API, and support for complex hierarchical inventory needs. Built on Django with a modern React frontend.



- **[metasfresh](https://github.com/metasfresh/metasfresh)**  

  Full open-source ERP system with strong inventory, warehouse, purchasing, and sales capabilities suitable for trade and industrial companies that need scalable stock management.



- **[Celerp](https://github.com/celerp/celerp)**  

  Self-hosted desktop/business management suite including multi-location inventory, barcode support, purchasing, manufacturing, and accounting modules.



- **[OpenQuarterMaster](https://github.com/Epic-Breakfast-Productions/OpenQuarterMaster)**  

  Open-source inventory management system designed to be simple yet powerful and extensible, with a core API and plugin architecture for specialized use cases.



- **[OpenStock](https://github.com/ospos-org/open-stock)**  

  Open-source stock controller and retail management API (Rust-based) covering inventory, purchase orders, POS-related operations, and transactions.



- **[MyCompany / lsFusion inventory solutions](https://mycompany.lsfusion.org/)**  

  Open-source inventory and ERP components focused on multi-warehouse stock, lot tracking, and integration with purchasing and sales.



- **[Other inventory & stock management projects](https://github.com/search?q=inventory+management+OR+stock+control+open+source)**  

  Additional community tools for part tracking, warehouse management, and simple retail inventory.



- **[ERPNext / Odoo inventory modules](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  

  Popular open-source ERPs with mature inventory, warehouse, and multi-location stock management capabilities that many retailers adapt.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Barcode & labeling**: Open tools for generating and scanning barcodes integrated with inventory systems.

- **Multi-channel connectors**: Community scripts and apps that sync stock between open inventory systems and Shopify, WooCommerce, or marketplaces.

- **Manufacturing / BOM extensions**: Plugins or modules that add bill-of-materials and production tracking on top of core inventory.

- **Reporting & forecasting**: Open BI tools (Metabase, Superset) or simple demand-planning notebooks layered on inventory data.

- **Mobile / warehouse apps**: Progressive web or mobile clients for receiving, picking, and cycle counting.

- Self-hosted stacks combining InvenTree or metasfresh with ecommerce platforms and open reporting tools.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

The strongest pure open-source inventory foundation is **InvenTree**, often extended with plugins and external integrations.  

For broader operational needs, **metasfresh**, **ERPNext**, or **Odoo** provide full ERP context including purchasing, sales, and accounting alongside inventory.  

Lightweight or specialized needs can be met with **Celerp**, **OpenQuarterMaster**, or **OpenStock**.  

Commercial platforms (Cin7, Katana, Zoho Inventory, Unleashed, Fishbowl, Finale, SkuVault, etc.) deliver polished multi-channel connectors, marketplace sync, support, and faster time-to-value.  

Many growing brands start with open-source or Zoho-level tools and later move to Cin7/Katana-class platforms as channel complexity and volume increase.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Inventory systems are operationally critical. Data accuracy, backup, access control, and integration reliability directly affect fulfillment and financial reporting.

- Open-source inventory platforms offer transparency and no per-user licensing but require hosting, maintenance, and integration work. Evaluate total cost of ownership, scalability, and support needs carefully before production use.
